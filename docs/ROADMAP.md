## Next

1. **Content round 2:** units 15–20: pretérito perfecto (he hecho), imperative (tú/usted,
   with pronouns), future and conditional, present subjunctive (wishes, advice, *cuando*),
   relative clauses and connectors (*aunque, sin embargo, por lo tanto*), *se* impersonal
   and passive. Verb engine: participles, future/conditional, subjunctive; unlock in the
   trainer.
2. **More texts:** 2–3 per level per focus topic (B1–B2 for work and news, A2–B1 for the
   lifestyle topics), and one A2 text per unit that uses that unit's grammar.
3. **Phase 5, writing:** short and long writing tasks (an email to a client, a summary of
   a text, an opinion paragraph) corrected by Gemini through the backend: corrections
   inline, a model answer, mistakes fed back into weak tags.
4. **Speaking:** shadowing (listen, repeat, compare) and read-aloud with the phone's
   speech recognition where available.
5. **Custom art theme:** if the owner supplies original artwork (background ~1290×2800 px,
   optional transparent PNG stickers), add it as a named theme.

## Inputs that make content better

Things the learners can give to make exercises sharper (see the chat history or ask):
- Real work material: anonymised quotes, delivery notes, site WhatsApp messages,
  emails to clients and suppliers, the words the team actually uses.
- Their mistakes: screenshots or notes of what they get wrong outside the app.
- A textbook they like (title and level) to mirror its sequence (no copying of pages).
- Articles they want to read, dropped into the Drive inbox.
- Goals and dates (a call in Spanish, an exam such as DELE B1) to aim units and texts.
